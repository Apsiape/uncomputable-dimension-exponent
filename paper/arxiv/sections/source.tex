\section{The finite programmed presentation}\label{sec:source}
Fix a finite deterministic counter program recognizing $K$. Counters take nonnegative integer values and instructions increment, decrement on a nonzero branch, or test zero. Such a recognizer follows from the classical universality of counter machines~\cite{SS63}. One direct construction stores the two halves of a Turing tape as integer-coded stacks. Pushing multiplies a stack code by a fixed base and adds a digit; popping computes quotient and remainder by that base. Increment, decrement and zero-test loops perform these operations using finitely many scratch counters. A unary input loop writes the integer input on the simulated tape. This gives one fixed finite instruction table. Add absorbing idle and halt controls.

The variables introduced below have the domains stated explicitly. Projection inclusions $Q\le F$ mean the polynomial equation $(I-F)Q=0$. A precision model satisfies every listed commutator and every displayed defining equation to precision $\delta$ in Definition~\ref{def:model}.

\subsection{Three compulsory clocks and a carry coordinate}
Use three copies of~\eqref{eq:clock}, with indices $a,f,t$ for address, fuel and time. Complete variable sets of different clocks commute. Add an address increment $V$ and a time increment $U$, satisfying~\eqref{eq:guard} for their own clocks. The address and fuel sets commute with $U$, and the time and fuel sets commute with $V$. Add a positive contraction $H$ with
\begin{align}
 H&=(I-P_a)+\tfrac14P_aT_aHT_a^*,\label{eq:carry-rec}\\
 [P_a,T_aHT_a^*]&=[H,A_a]=0,\qquad
 V^*A_aV=A_a-I+\tfrac74H.\label{eq:carry-step}
\end{align}
Include $H$ in the address set, commuting with all fuel and time variables. The last equality will control many address translations without an exponential precision cost.

\subsection{Address decoding and reset}
Put $P_{a,1}=T_aP_aT_a^*$, $E_{00}=(I-P_a)(I-P_{a,1})$ and $E_1=P_a(I-P_{a,1})$. Add a projection $L$ and a positive contraction $R$ with
\begin{align}
 L&=E_1+E_{00}T_a^2LT_a^{-2},\label{eq:decode-L}\\
 R&=(I-E_{00})+\tfrac12E_{00}T_a^2RT_a^{-2}.\label{eq:decode-R}
\end{align}
The products are polynomials even before the approximate digits commute. In exact models the cylinder
\begin{equation}\label{eq:address-cyl}
 B_e=\left(\prod_{j<e}T_a^{2j}E_{00}T_a^{-2j}\right)
       T_a^{2e}E_1T_a^{-2e}
\end{equation}
selects first nonzero base-four address digit one in position $e$.
Here each base-four address digit groups two successive binary clock bits, with the first bit least significant.

Add a reset projection $Z$, set $S=T_fT_t$, and impose
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reset}
 Z=(I-P_f)+P_f(I-P_t)SZS^*.
\end{equation}
In exact models write $Q_j^{f}=T_f^jP_fT_f^{-j}$, $P_{t,j}=T_t^jP_tT_t^{-j}$ and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fuel-cyl}
 C_k=\left(\prod_{j<k}Q_j^{f}\right)(I-Q_k^{f}),\qquad
 D_k=\prod_{j<k}(I-P_{t,j}).
\end{equation}
The product defining $C_k$ means $(\prod_{j<k}Q_j^f)(I-Q_k^f)$, with empty product $I$.

\subsection{Counters and transitions}
Encode counter value $n$ by $r=2^{-n}$. For each counter add a positive contraction $r_i$, a zero-test projection $z_i$ and a positive contraction $s_i$ with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:zero-test}
 r_i=z_i+\tfrac12(I-z_i)s_i.
\end{equation}
All instantaneous workspace variables commute. For each control state $\sigma$, add a projection $q_\sigma$, with $q_\sigma q_\tau=0$ for $\sigma\ne\tau$ and $\sum_\sigma q_\sigma=I$. Each program branch selector is $q_\sigma$, $q_\sigma z_i$ or $q_\sigma(I-z_i)$; these selectors partition $I$. The next control is the sum of selectors entering that state. The next counter is the sum, over selectors, of that selector times $r_i/2$, $r_i$ or $2r_i$ as its instruction dictates. The factor two is used only on a nonzero decrement branch. These prescriptions define a fixed rational polynomial map $\Step$ on counters and controls.

Initialization is
\[
 \Init(q_{\mathrm{start}})=L,\quad \Init(q_{\mathrm{idle}})=I-L,\quad
 \Init(r_1)=R,\quad \Init(r_i)=I\ (i>1),
\]
with other controls zero. For each counter or control $W$, impose
\begin{align}
 ZW&=Z\Init(W),\label{eq:initialize}\\
 U^*WU&=Z_+\Init(W)+(I-Z_+)\Step(W),\qquad Z_+=U^*ZU.\label{eq:transition}
\end{align}
The witnesses and zero-tests satisfy~\eqref{eq:zero-test} at every time; separate update equations for them are unnecessary. Add a flag projection $F$ and require
\begin{equation}\label{eq:halt-flag}
 q_{\mathrm{halt}}\le F.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Commutations and the conditional clock}
The following is the complete additional commutation list.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item $L,R,F$ commute with each other, with $A_a$, and with every fuel and time clock variable, including $U$.
\item $Z$ and all instantaneous workspace variables commute with one another and with $A_a,A_f,A_t,L,R,F$.
\item The address set, including $V,H$, commutes with the complete fuel and time sets.
\end{itemize}
Workspace is not required to commute with $U$ or the address shifts and flips.

For the conditional clock add positive contractions $B,C$, a projection $Q$ and a self-adjoint unitary $Y$. Impose
\begin{align}
 Q&\le F,\qquad [B,Q]=[B,F]=[C,Q]=[C,F]=[B,C]=0,\label{eq:conditional-comm}\\
 VBV^*&=4B-3Q,\qquad VCV^*=4C-3F,\label{eq:conditional-affine}\\
 YFY&=F,\qquad YQY=F-Q,\label{eq:conditional-flip}\\
 [Y,VBV^*]&=0,\qquad [Y,C]=0.\label{eq:conditional-tail}
\end{align}
Each of $B,C,Q,Y$ commutes with $A_a$ and every fuel and time clock variable, including $U$. Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:optional-translates}
 F_i=V^iFV^{-i},\qquad Q_i=V^iQV^{-i},\qquad Y_i=V^iYV^{-i}.
\end{equation}
The intended ternary measurement is $(I-F_i,F_i-Q_i,Q_i)$. Its inactive outcome contributes no required bit; its two active outcomes are exchanged by $Y_i$. The coordinate $C$ records the flags. It is needed to control commutation of their translates, which commutation with $A_a$ alone would not imply.

This finishes the finite presentation, denoted $\mathcal P_K$. Every branch and every variable used above occurs in a finite list; $e,k,i$ in later arguments index consequences, not additional defining relations.
